## **SYNOPSIS**

In a world where monsters roam the wild lands, the true heart of adventure lies beneath the earth. Ancient labyrinths, known simply as The Dungeons, the remnants of a forgotten age.

You lead a fellowship of adventurers on a floor-by-floor descent. There is no grand prophecy or complex political intrigue; there is only the torchlight, the sound of steel on stone, and the drive to discover what lies at the bottom of the next staircase.
